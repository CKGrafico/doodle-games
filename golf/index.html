Mouse: hold left click to charge power, then release to play. Right click during charging cancels. Keyboard and touch actions retain their direct controls. Aim locks while charging and the trajectory follows the power. Swing and Space use the selected power.

A QUICK CADDIE NOTE

Play the course.

Camera views: top, third person and first person. In first person, move the mouse horizontally to turn the shot direction, hold Shift for fine aim, and use the wheel for power. Aim stays fixed during charging. Lock mouse allows unrestricted turning; Esc releases it. Other views retain point-at-ground aiming. Switching views cancels input.

Touch: drag on the playing surface to refine aim, then use the power control and action button. Dragging never launches a shot.

  1. Aim your line. Move the mouse across the course or use A and D (left and right arrows also work). On touch screens, tap the course to aim, then tap Swing. The dotted arc previews the shot, including wind.
  2. Choose enough club. Driver for distance, iron for approaches, wedge for height and bunkers, putter on the green. Adjust power with W and S or the up and down arrows. E or right-click cycles clubs.
  3. Count every stroke. Water and out of bounds add one penalty stroke and replay from the previous spot. The ball must finish in the cup.

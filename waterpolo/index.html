A QUICK POOL BRIEFING

Keep the ball moving.

Touch: use the left stick gently for slower movement, or push it fully to sprint. Keep the same finger on the stick while using action buttons with the other hand.

  1. Swim into space. You control the blue player marked YOU. Movement help holds shape until you use WASD.
  2. Beat the clock. Space or click passes, E or right-click leads a teammate, and Q shoots. Your team has 30 seconds to attempt a goal.
  3. Win possession. Press F near the ball carrier to steal. Tab switches your player. Goalkeepers protect three-metre goals. Four quick quarters decide the match.

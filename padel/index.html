A QUICK COURT BRIEFING

Play the bounce.

Cameras: use View to choose Top view, 3rd person or 1st person. Switching cancels unfinished input and keeps your current game. First person follows the player or shot position; the mouse still aims normally.

Touch: use the left stick gently for slower movement, or push it fully to sprint. Keep the same finger on the stick while using action buttons with the other hand.

Padel is doubles, with a net and playable walls. You control the blue player marked YOU. Your partner covers the other side.

  1. Serve diagonally. Press Hit. Your player bounces the ball and serves underarm. The return must bounce first.
  2. Ground, then glass. Let the ball rebound off your walls, then hit before its second ground bounce. Hitting the opponents’ wall before their ground loses the point.
  3. Find the space. Aim with the mouse or tap the far court. Hold Space to return, E for a high lob, or Q for a smash when the ball is high.

Movement help automatically positions you; directional input always takes over. On a touch screen, use the movement pad and shot buttons; SWITCH changes your partner. Push the stick fully to sprint (Shift on keyboard).

Quick match: first to 3 games, deciding point at 40–40. One set: advantage scoring, 7-point tie-break at 6–6. Warm-up has no match winner. This is an arcade adaptation: assisted strokes and no out-of-court retrieval.

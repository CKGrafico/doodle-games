THE MATCHDAY BRIEFING

Find the extra pass.

Cameras: View selects top, third person or first person. First person: move the mouse to look around; WASD moves relative to that view and the centre ring aims your shot. Lock mouse allows unrestricted turning; Esc releases it. Hold left click to charge, release to play; right click cancels. Switching views cancels input.

Touch: use the left stick gently for slower movement, or push it fully to sprint. Keep the same finger on the stick while using action buttons with the other hand.

You control the blue player marked YOU. Ten teammates move, defend, and make runs. Red fields a full eleven too.

  1. Build your attack. Press Space or click to pass to a teammate in space. E or right-click plays a lofted pass or cross. K plays a through ball. Control follows your receiver.
  2. Take your chance. Q shoots toward goal (J also works). Move the mouse to choose your corner; without a mouse, your player aims automatically. Hold Shift while moving to sprint.
  3. Win it back. Tab switches to a nearby defender. F tackles when you are close. Movement help positions your player until you take over with WASD.

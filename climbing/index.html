How to race

  • Wait for GO. Moving during the countdown is a false start.
  • Aim at a higher hold. Hold left click and release when the meter reaches enough power. Right click cancels.
  • Short moves are safe. Skipping a hold can be faster, but needs more power and consumes more grip. Excess power also wastes grip.
  • Clean moves build Flow. Missing or overreaching causes a short auto-belay recovery and resets the combo.
  • Left/right or up/down changes the target. Hold Space or the MOVE button to charge, then release. P pauses and ? opens help.
  • This is an arcade race inspired by the 15 m parallel format, not the licensed standard route. It omits official brackets, route dimensions and equipment certification.

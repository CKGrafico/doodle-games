Find your line.

Pass between each pair of flags. Turn before you reach the gate. Hold BRAKE or S / down arrow to slow down and control the line. Hold and release HOP to jump over rocks. Centre your steering for landing. Missing a gate adds three seconds; a fall adds two. Hard turns at high speed drain balance. Cross the finish at 850 metres. This is an arcade course, not official competition scoring.

Touch: steer with your left thumb and hold the action with your right. The charge meter shows the power. Release once to act. BRAKE can be held independently. Keyboard: A / D or arrows steer, S / down brakes, hold and release Space for the action. Mouse: point left or right to steer, hold and release left click to act, right click cancels. P pauses.

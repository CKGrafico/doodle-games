# Rules research and arcade adaptations

The games use official dimensions and rule concepts, then deliberately simplify them for quick browser play.

## Football

- [IFAB Law 1: The Field of Play](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-field-of-play/) informed the 105 by 68 metre pitch, 7.32 by 2.44 metre goals, penalty areas, goal areas, centre circle, and corner arcs.
- [IFAB Law 3: The Players](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players/) informed two teams of 11 including one goalkeeper each.
- [IFAB Law 11: Offside](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/offside/) informed receiver-based offside and restart exemptions.

The game omits fouls, cards, injuries, substitutions, added time, penalties, and extra time. Tackles always use the clean arcade outcome.

## Golf

- [The R&A Rules of Golf](https://www.randa.org/rog/the-rules-of-golf) informed stroke play, playing the ball as it lies, and stroke-and-distance relief.
- [USGA Rule 17](https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/rules/rules-2019/rules-of-golf/rule-17.html) informed the one-stroke penalty for water relief.

The game uses compact fictional holes, reduced distances, four representative clubs, a visible trajectory preview, and immediate replay from the previous spot after water or out of bounds.

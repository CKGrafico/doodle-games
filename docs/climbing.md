# Climbing race

Research checked on 2026-09-08 before implementation:

- [World Climbing speed overview](https://www.worldclimbing.com/speed/index): competitors race on identical parallel routes to reach the top of a 15 m wall and hit the finish pad first.
- [2026 World Climbing event regulations](https://images.ifsc-climbing.org/ifsc/image/private/t_q_good/prd/brsjsyh2ea4fxinl8nsa.pdf): individual speed uses two parallel lanes, an audible start signal, false starts/falls as non-valid outcomes and measured completion times.

The game uses an original route and enlarged, readable holds. It is an arcade interpretation rather than a licensed standard speed wall. Charged movement, grip, Flow and recoverable auto-belay falls are game mechanics, not official scoring rules. Official bracket structures, certified geometry, equipment requirements and full false-start/timing procedure are omitted and disclosed in help and MECHANICS.md.

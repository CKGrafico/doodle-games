Read the wave.

Cameras: use View to choose Top view, 3rd person or 1st person. Switching cancels unfinished input and keeps your current game. First person follows the player or shot position; the mouse still aims normally.

  1. Earn your points. Cross at least three metres of wave face before reversing for a cutback. Hold CARVE while turning to perform a snap.
  2. Ride the barrel. Enter the marked tube on the right and hold a steady line inside it for 1.25 seconds.
  3. Use the lip. Pump low on the face to build speed. Near the right lip the action becomes LAUNCH. The marked air sections give longer airtime and a scoring bonus.
  4. Finish the trick. Steer while airborne to rotate, hold GRAB for style, then let go of steering to straighten before touching down. Too much rotation on landing causes a wipeout.
  5. Protect your combo. Different tricks build bigger combinations. Five seconds without another trick banks the points; falling first loses the pending combo. There are no points for simply waiting.
  6. Three waves. Choose your first break; the heat visits all three. Your best two wave scores count. The best heat is stored only on this device.

Controls. A / D or left/right arrows steer. S / down arrow holds CARVE on the surface or GRAB in the air. Hold Space and release for the charged action. Mouse: point at your line, hold and release left click to act; centre the pointer to straighten in the air. Right click cancels. Touch: left thumb steers, right thumb holds and releases the action; the second button carves or grabs. P pauses, ? opens help.

Find your line.

Stay inside the buoy lines. The red ring marks the moving wave pocket: stay near it for speed and points. Turn back after carving at least three metres to build Flow. Hold and release PUMP to gain speed; near either lip, with enough speed, it launches an air. Centre your steering to land. Hard turns at high speed drain balance. Wipeouts reset Flow but you recover on the same wave. Each wave lasts 25 seconds.

Touch: steer with your left thumb and hold the action with your right. The charge meter shows the power. Release once to act. BRAKE can be held independently. Keyboard: A / D or arrows steer, S / down brakes, hold and release Space for the action. Mouse: point left or right to steer, hold and release left click to act, right click cancels. P pauses.
